FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Hospitality Procurement FAQ

What are the Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)?
• Smart Sachets & Mouth Freshener: 4,000 Sachets.
• Butter Paper Sheets: 30 KG.
• Printed Paper Cups: 3,000 Pcs.
• Tissue Papers: 250 Packets (500 Packets for custom logo printing).
• Chopsticks: 2,000 Pairs.
• QR Stands: No MOQ (Single pieces available).
Are printing plate / dye charges paid every time?
No! Dye charges (₹600 for Single Colour, ₹2,500 for Multi-Colour) are a strictly one-time initial investment. For all your future repeat batches, dye charges are ₹0.
How do the QR Codes on Smart Sachets work?
We generate and print a dynamic QR code on the back of each sachet that directly opens your Google Business Review link, Instagram profile, or digital food menu without the diner having to search or type your name.
What are the official payment and dispatch terms?
As stated on our official bills: 50% advance upon digital mock-up confirmation and 50% balance before factory dispatch. Goods are dispatched directly from our Indore atelier.
